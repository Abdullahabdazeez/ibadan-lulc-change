# Charts — Ibadan Land-Cover Change and Urban Expansion

These charts summarise the main quantitative evidence from my analysis of Ibadan's land-cover change between 2013 and 2023. They are designed to complement the maps by making the scale, direction and reliability of the observed changes easy to interpret.

## Chart series

| Chart | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Land-cover area comparison** | Compares the mapped area of Built-up, Vegetation, Water and Bare soil in 2013 and 2023. |
| **Land-cover change summary** | Shows the direction and magnitude of net change for each class. |
| **Project overview** | Brings the headline land-cover findings together in a compact visual summary. |
| **Independent validation** | Presents the key performance measures from the locked holdout evaluation. |

## Headline result

Built-up land increased from **99.866 km²** to **330.177 km²**, while Vegetation declined by **230.331 km²**. The strongest mapped transition was **Vegetation → Built-up (246.104 km²)**.

## Reading the figures

The charts should be interpreted together with the spatial outputs. Area totals explain **how much** change occurred; the maps show **where** it occurred. Validation metrics describe classification performance and should not be interpreted as measures of the magnitude of urban growth.

## Presentation standard

All public-facing figures use clear titles, labelled units, concise captions and consistent terminology. Technical diagnostics are documented separately so that this folder remains focused on the figures used to communicate the project findings.
