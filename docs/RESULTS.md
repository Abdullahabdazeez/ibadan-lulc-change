# Results

Built-up land increased from **604.85 km² (18.86%)** in 2013 to **1,284.49 km² (40.06%)** in 2023, a net increase of **679.64 km²** or **112.36%**. Vegetation declined from **2,584.05 km² (80.59%)** to **1,894.84 km² (59.10%)**, representing a net loss of **689.21 km²**.

The transition matrix identified **723.18 km² of vegetation converted to built-up land**, making it the dominant conversion pathway. Built-up persistence covered **557.16 km²**, while **1,849.84 km²** remained vegetated across both observations.

The 2013 Random Forest classification achieved **92.34% overall accuracy** and **0.895 Kappa**. The 2023 model achieved **97.04% overall accuracy** and **0.961 Kappa**.
