# Limitations

- Landsat’s 30 m pixels can mix several urban land-cover types within one cell.
- Dynamic World begins after the 2013 observation year, so stable recent classes and spectral rules were used to strengthen the 2013 reference sample.
- Water and bare-soil classes occupy small proportions of the mapped area and are sensitive to seasonal conditions and spectral confusion.
- The results show land-cover conversion, not parcel-level land-use functions or development legality.
- The analysis should be supplemented with field validation and higher-resolution imagery before site-specific regulatory decisions are made.
